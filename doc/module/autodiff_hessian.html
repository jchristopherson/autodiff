public subroutine hessian(fcn, x, H, g, f, work, args, nthreads)

Compute a scalar function's Hessian, and optionally its gradient and value, by seeding hyper-dual inputs. The Hessian is symmetric and requires evaluations of fcn; the gradient and value are taken from the diagonal evaluations at no additional cost.

public subroutine hvp(fcn, x, v, Hv, work, args)

Evaluate the product of a scalar function's Hessian and a vector without forming the Hessian. Each evaluation seeds the first derivative direction with v and the second with a unit vector, so the mixed derivative yields one component of . This requires size(x) evaluations of fcn.
